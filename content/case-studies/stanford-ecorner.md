## The site

eCorner is the media library of the Stanford Technology Ventures Program (STVP). It publishes talks, podcasts and articles about entrepreneurship and innovation.

- The home page shows recent episodes, each as a video or a podcast.
- Episodes are grouped into series, such as Entrepreneurial Thought Leaders, Stanford Innovation Lab and Move Fast and Fix the Planet.
- Each series links to Apple Podcasts, Spotify and an RSS feed.
- Visitors can browse all episodes, find contributors and read articles.
- The site runs on Drupal 11. The response headers say so.

Live site: [stvp.stanford.edu/ecorner](https://stvp.stanford.edu/ecorner/)

## My role

I led frontend development and served as tech lead on Stanford eCorner, the media library of the Stanford Technology Ventures Program, while I was a Senior Frontend Engineer at 10up, between January 2021 and April 2022.
