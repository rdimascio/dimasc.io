## The site

holtinternational.org is the website of Holt International. Holt describes itself as a Christian organization that cares for vulnerable children around the world.

- Visitors can sponsor a child, donate, and shop seasonal gifts, such as a Christmas gift or a meal for a family.
- A "Meet Waiting Children" area shows profiles of children who wait for adoptive families.
- The site explains four programs: family strengthening, care for orphaned and vulnerable children, adoption, and post-adoption services.
- Supporters can sign in to a "My Holt" account.
- The site runs on WordPress, hosted on Kinsta behind Cloudflare. The page assets and response headers say so.

Live site: [holtinternational.org](https://www.holtinternational.org/)
