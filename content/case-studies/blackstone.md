## The site

blackstone.com is the public website of Blackstone. The site describes the firm as the world's largest alternative asset manager.

- The home page opens with "Build with Blackstone" and a carousel of featured stories, such as the Inside Blackstone podcast.
- The main sections are The Firm, What We Do, News & Insights, Financial Advisors and Shareholders.
- There are separate paths for limited partners, shareholders and financial advisors.
- A sign-up form lets visitors get insights and firm news by email.
- The site runs on WordPress behind Cloudflare. The generator tag and the response headers say so.

Live site: [blackstone.com](https://www.blackstone.com/)
