## The site

serhant.com is the website of SERHANT., a real estate brokerage. The listings page lets buyers search homes across the brokerage's regions.

- The page opens with "Your New Home Awaits" and a list of regions, from Arizona to Washington D.C.
- Featured properties show price, address, beds, baths and square feet.
- Buyers can filter by beds, baths, size, price and property type, such as condo, co-op, townhouse, land and commercial.
- A separate section shows SERHANT. Signature listings, the brokerage's $10M+ luxury division.
- Visitors can also browse rentals, new development and agents.
- The response headers name Luxury Presence as the platform, behind Cloudflare.

Live site: [serhant.com](https://serhant.com/home-search/listings/). The listings URL now opens the Buy page.
