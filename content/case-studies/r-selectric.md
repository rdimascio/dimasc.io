## The site

r-selectric.com is the website of R & S Electric, a family-owned electrical contractor in Arlington, Washington. The site says the company has served its community since 1990.

- The home page opens with "More than just another electrical contractor." and two calls to action: start a project or learn about the team.
- Three service areas are described: multi-family, commercial and residential.
- Customer reviews and a five-star rating appear on the home page.
- A phone number sits in the header, and the footer lists the address, phone and email.
- The site is built with Astro and served through Cloudflare. It uses Cloudflare Turnstile for bot checks. The generator tag and page scripts say so.

Live site: [r-selectric.com](https://r-selectric.com/)
