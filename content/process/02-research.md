before i build anything, i read up on the tools you already pay for + the other options. a lot of the time the fix is a setting or something you already own.

if something has to move, like your site to a new host, i look into the move itself: what comes over, how long it takes and what could go wrong. you get what i found w/ links, and i flag anything i couldn't confirm.
