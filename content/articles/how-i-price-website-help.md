most small businesses don't need a developer every day. some months it's nothing, then one week the booking link breaks and everything is on fire. so i made 3 options instead of one.

## care, $199/month

for when your site mostly works and you just want someone watching it.

- updates, done for you
- uptime + security monitoring
- up to 2 small fixes a month
- i reply within 1 business day

cancel anytime.

## monthly help, $1,500/month

for when you have a list. new pages, a form that should talk to your email tool, a report you keep building by hand. you add jobs to one list and i work through them, one at a time. most small jobs are done in 2 business days.

pause or cancel anytime. not happy in the first 7 days? cancel and i refund 75% of your first payment.

## one project

for one bigger job, like a new website. we talk first, i look at what you have, then you get one price per step before i start. you can stop after any step and keep what's done.

## how to pick

> if you can't name 3 things you'd want done this month, start w/ care.

if you've got a list, go monthly. if it's one big thing w/ a clear end, go one project. not sure? email me and i'll tell you which one makes sense, or if you need me at all.
